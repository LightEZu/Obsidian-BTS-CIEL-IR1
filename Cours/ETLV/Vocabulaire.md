- Cybercrime : A cybercrime is a criminal activity in wich a **computer, network or digital device** is an **essential part** of the crime.
- Malware : An evil software, a virus, infection of components, software to harm a device
- Cyberbullying : Online harrassement
- Social Engineering : Collecting data about people in order to access sensitive data
- Phising : Fraudulent mail or text to trick you into giving informations
- Cryptocurrency : Virtual money allowing people to transfer money to each other through an online system
- Darknet : Can be access via TOR Browser, illegal marketplace, a way to comunicate without being tracked.
- Hacker : Person gaining access of your computer without having your consent.
